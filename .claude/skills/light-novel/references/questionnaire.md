# Building the bible from the "3,000 Light Novel Questions" artifact

The creator answers the questionnaire in a Claude artifact. Its answers live in the artifact's
database, not in the HTML, so pull them with `ArtifactData`, digest them once, and read slices.
Total cost: one artifact read (~15k tokens of HTML you can ignore), one database dump, one digest.

## Steps

1. `Artifact` `list` → find the questionnaire's URL. `Artifact` `read` it once (the read is required
   before `ArtifactData` will serve the database; don't study the HTML).
2. `ArtifactData` `list`, `collection: "answers"`, `query: {"limit": 1000}`, `out_dir: <scratchpad>/ans`.
   Each document `sNNN` is one section of the questionnaire.
3. `python3 .claude/skills/light-novel/scripts/digest.py build <scratchpad>/ans/answers <scratchpad>/digest.txt`
4. Read slices by part (below) with `digest.py slice <digest> s060-s069 --cols 350`. Read only the
   parts you need for the current job. The whole digest is ~70 KB; never read it in one go.
5. `Write` `BIBLE.md`, `outline.md` (one line per chapter, beats for the next chapter) and `STATE.md`.
   Everything the creator left blank or contradictory goes in STATE under "Confirm with creator".

## Reading the answers

- Many answers were generated by "Let Claude decide" and contradict each other (three different
  openings, two titles, a 3,000-word target next to "extra long"). The creator's rule (s192) is
  *ask which wins*. Pick one for the draft, list every such pick in STATE and in the chapter report.
- Answers in `[+...]` are the creator's own "other" text and outrank the chip they clicked.
- The chapter sections (Part 19) often hold a full scene-by-scene plan in the "What happens" answer.
  Use it as the beat sheet; don't re-plan what is already planned.
- Look for the small canon the creator typed personally (character names, catchphrases, the
  closing line of a chapter, "no boys' love", "the romance never happens in a child's body").
  Those are the lines a reader of the questionnaire will check first.
