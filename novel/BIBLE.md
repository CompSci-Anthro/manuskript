## Premise
- Logline: Kael, 18, dies under a falling cherry tree in Japan; the God of the Past grants his last wish ("I wish I was ten again") by sending him to Tutankhamun's Egypt in a ten-year-old's body. Arrested as a foreign spy, he is made the boy king's playmate, and must survive the court on an adult mind alone.
- Series: Throne of Ashes (title = his original future: death in two years). Vol 1: *The Cursed Playmate*. Ending (author-only): Tut is assassinated; Ay takes the throne as history records; Kael stays the unseen hand.
- Genre / tone: historical (main) + romance + light supernatural; shōnen/seinen, 18+. Dark, tense, bittersweet, serious (comedy ~1/10, never during deaths). Beautiful but dangerous. Mood colour #5c0701, blazing afternoon heat, a gong.
- Target length: 10–20 vols. Vol 1 ≈ 70,000 words: 9 chapters + epilogue, 3–5 scenes each, 5–8k words/chapter; Ch1 extra long. Every chapter ends on a cliffhanger.

## Cast
- **Kael Amunet** — hero, narrator. 18 inside, looks 10. 130 cm, slim, fair, blond short wavy hair in sharp layers, blue eyes, scar over LEFT eye (fencing tournament), right-handed, frayed linen-cord bracelet woven by his mother. In Japan: black hair, black school uniform. Arrives in a blue shirt with red sleeves. Voice: high and bright, but formal, measured, archaic, clipped; faint foreign lilt. Doesn't swear. Tics: "They see a child." / "The gods don't whisper to fools." / scared: "Of course. Just another variable I didn't account for." Counts steps, maps rooms, unravels threads when planning, flinches at his reflection in still water. Brave, smart, proud, serious; arrogant, lies easily, can't ask for help. Former fencer: perfect technique, no strength. Wants control of his own fate; needs one person who sees the real him. Lies: claims to be an orphan raised by nomads. Past: historian parents died in an arson house fire 3 yrs ago (culprit never caught; mother whispered a warning: "Don't let them forget his name."). Twin brother Soren lives with an uncle in Osaka; Kael lives alone with the smoke-stained books.
- **Tutankhamun** — 9, newly crowned. Fine-boned, tan, dark weary eyes, nemes + uraeus, wide gold collar with lapis inlay, tied-on false beard; feet barely reach the footstool. Few, flat words in public; eager only in private. Calls himself "Pharaoh" when angry. Touches his father's obsidian scarab brooch when afraid; fidgets with his collar when anxious. Lonely, perceptive, braver than his advisors think. Private name for Kael (later): "Khenti." Loves senet.
- **Amenhotep the Vizier** — 45, villain. Tall, gaunt, shaved head with gold ornament, hazel eyes, ritual scars, linen-wrapped scarred fingers with many gold rings (one blackened thumbnail), indigo robes with gold Amun glyphs, lapis-and-carnelian collar, falcon-headed staff. Never raises his voice; threats sound like kindness. "The child's eyes betray him." / group motto "Order before mercy." Plays senet alone; tips the cheap piece off the board. Never harms the king openly; always has a scapegoat. Ch1: says nothing to Kael; marks him. Keeps the 10,000-yen bill.
- **Nakhtmin** — 22, Commander of the Royal Retainers and Shield of the Living Pharaoh. Broad, scarred jaw, black hair pulled back severely, old eyes. Cold, flat; speaks only orders and reports; "It will be done." Sketches formations in dust. Answers to the Vizier.
- **Nakhti** (Ch2) — 40, mentor, "the Raven of Kemet." Ex-slave royal advisor, ink-stained, green eyes, one eye scarred shut, wolf-tail grey hair. Answers questions with questions. "The Nile remembers what men forget." Weakness: drink.
- **Aneb** (Ch2–3) — 18, palace slave who raised Tut's days; 6'1", burn scar across face. Low, calm, few words, interrupts, rude, swears most. Calls Kael "Boy." Jealous rival → ally.
- **Kalici** (Ch3) — 10, servant girl sent by the Vizier to frame Kael; can't scream. Rambling, apologetic, soft. "What mask are you hiding today?" Loyalty, never romance.
- **Ren** (Ch9 only) — gold mask, black leather, jewelled sword; "My name is Ren. A prophecy told me to save you."
- **Wennefer** — High Priest of Amun, 32, measured pauses.
- **Hori** — 20, river soldier; sunburnt nose; arrests Kael, leaves him the bread, "That one lasted eleven days." Not cruel; expects nothing. **Henut** — bread seller, cat tattoo. **Userhat** — heavy courtier who keeps the king's house; objects on cue.
- **The God of the Past** — ancient, unreadable; dusty gold, drifting sand, white-on-black. Speaks little: "Simple? We shall see."
- **Mrs. Hayashi** — old tea-shop owner on the cherry road; studied texts with Kael's parents; holds their unpaid book order.

## World rules
- Setting: Thebes (Waset), Kemet, c. 1332 BCE, start of Tutankhamun's reign. History wins; story lives in the gaps. Real names, common English spellings.
- No magic. Only supernatural forces: the God of the Past and the curse. Kael wins by mind and sacrifice, never strength.
- Curse/clock: two years (two floods); then the past lets go and Kael returns to the moment beneath the tree (death). Language: he understands and is understood, but the words land a half beat late and his accent marks him foreign.
- No execution until the Weighing of the Heart is performed before the pharaoh (priest, balance, feather of Ma'at); the Vizier cannot skip it. Kael knows this rite only as an afterlife scene; here it's done to the living.
- Money: barter and deben weights of copper. Police: Medjay. Outsiders: "khat-su" (sand-foreign). Courtiers kiss the ground before the king. Tut's footstool is painted with bound foreigners.
- Kael's knowledge = what a well-read student of historian parents knows, gaps and mistakes included (Amarna, Akhenaten, Restoration Stela, Tut dies at ~19, Ay succeeds). He doesn't recognise a vizier named Amenhotep.
- Hard nevers: nothing sexual or romantic involving anyone under 18 or in a child's body (Kael included); no boys' love; no real-world politics; no fan service; no suicide/self-harm.

## Style sheet
- POV / tense: first person, Kael, PRESENT tense. Interludes (other POVs) only at chapter level (e.g. Ch3 opens on Kalici/Amenhotep).
- Voice: clean and quick, easy reading level; lyrical but cold; few metaphors; sensory (Nile mud, myrrh, sweat). Kael keeps a ledger of debts. Constant contrast child body / adult mind. Modern words OK ("minutes", "okay"); modern units.
- Paragraphs: often a single line. Dialogue ~40%, realistic, heavy subtext, "……" silences, key lines alone on a line. Thoughts in *italics*. No fourth wall, no pop-culture jokes, no Japanese words in dialogue, no named attacks.
- Violence: shown directly, slow, vivid, painful. Swearing rare and real.
- Headers: place/time header only when place or time jumps (format: **Place. Time.**). `***` for scene breaks inside a file.
- Honorifics & address: "Majesty", "Lord of the Two Lands", "Master"; courtiers call Kael "the Pharaoh's Toy". No -san/-sama.
- Recurring terms (exact): Kemet, Thebes/Waset, Ma'at, Duat, Ammit, senet, deben, Medjay, ka, ba, uraeus, nemes, khat-su, Hatti. Exclamations: "By the Duat's teeth!", "Ammit take it." Blessing: "May the Nile carry you home." Group oath (later): "What flows returns."
