# Ch02 — The Shield of the Pharaoh (day 2)

Aneb (Tut's burn-scarred slave, jealous, "Boy") brings Kael to Tut's bedchamber. Senet: Kael loses slowly; Tut laughs after checking the door; Kael eats only after Aneb tastes. An assassin comes through the servants' door behind the curtain, stops a hand's width from Tut and looks to the door; Nakhtmin cuts his throat. "That's the third this season. Your turn." Aneb sends Kael to "the Raven." Nakhti takes him on for one price: never lie to him. At dinner ("Watch this common occurrence") Kanefer's cup arrives already full; he dies into the lotus bowl; nobody stops eating; Amenhotep smiles at the widow, then at Kael's unchildlike face; Tut pats Kael's head to make him a pet again. Nakhti's lesson: the stones of power, three truths, two Egypts, the hooded man. Kael: the temple's grain grew "since the old king died." Ends on Nakhti's line.
