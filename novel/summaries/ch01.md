# Ch01 — The Wish Beneath the Cherry Blossoms (day 1)

Cold open on the reed mat: "They say gods don't make mistakes." Japan, wet April: Kael (18, fencing scar, mother's red cord; parents dead in an unsolved arson; twin Soren in Osaka) wishes aloud to be ten again. The oldest cherry tree falls on him. The God of the Past grants it: ten years old, two floods of the Nile, then back under the tree; he keeps the ¥10,000 bill and the cord, gets an hourglass, hears his mother's voice: "Don't let them forget his name." Thebes: arrested as Khasut, taken by Hori to the west-bank palace. The old fan-bearer teaches him to kneel, then is weighed and killed. Kael says "Nebkheperure"; Tut sentences him: "my royal playmate and pet." The bill goes into the Vizier's cedar box. "Learn slowly." Servants' room: "That one lasted eleven days"; a sandal; ten and a half scratches. "One. Seven hundred and twenty-nine to go."
