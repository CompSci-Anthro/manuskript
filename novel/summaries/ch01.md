Ch01 — The Wish Beneath the Cherry Blossoms. Kael, 18, alone since an arson fire killed his historian parents, walks the cherry road; Mrs. Hayashi holds his mother's six-book order (he pockets a 10,000-yen bill to pay after school). At the oldest tree's sealed shrine he wishes to be ten again; the tree falls and kills him. The God of the Past grants it: two years (two floods), understood tongue, keeps only bill and bracelet; speaks his mother's last words, "Don't let them forget his name." Kael wakes in Thebes, blond and blue-eyed, ten; tries to pay for bread with the bill; flees, caught in Nile mud. In the throne room an overseer is "weighed" (rigged scale) and killed. Tut, nine, declares Kael his playmate and pet. Amenhotep marks him and keeps the bill. War news from Hatti. Tut: "You looked at him before you looked at me." Soldier: the last playmate lasted eleven days.
