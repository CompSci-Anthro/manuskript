## Ch08 — The Slave Who Heard Too Much, and the Blood That Will Not Wash Off
Summary: Kael's narration returns. The morning after Kalici smells myrrh at the foot of the king's bed, Kael and Nakhtmin search the bedchamber and find a loose grate over the ceiling vent, the way the hooded man came in. Kael keeps sleeping there anyway, because moving would warn the Vizier. Wennefer, the High Priest of Amun, arrives to "bless" the king's foreign playmate and corners Kael about knowledge no child should have. Kael gives him a partial truth. That night in Tut's chambers, Kael, Tut, Aneb and Nakhtmin hold their first secret council: Nakhtmin admits he will spy on the Vizier from inside, and they agree to join forces against him. Aneb argues with Kael the whole time. A young water-carrier slave has heard everything from behind the curtain and runs toward the Vizier's wing. Kael chases him into a dark corridor and kills him with his dagger before he can reach the door. Aneb finds him kneeling over the body. They hide it together without a word. At dawn, word spreads that Wennefer was taken to the Vizier's house for questioning and that a masked stranger broke him out. Kael scrubs his hands until they bleed. That night he lies down at the foot of the king's bed again and hears the vent grate creak.
- Scene 1: The Grate. Kael and Nakhtmin search Tut's bedchamber and find the loose ceiling-vent grate.
- Scene 2: The Blessing. Wennefer corners Kael about his impossible knowledge, and Kael gives him a partial truth.
- Scene 3: The Council. Kael, Tut, Aneb and Nakhtmin agree to join forces against the Vizier, and Aneb clashes with Kael.
- Scene 4: The Curtain. A slave overhears, runs, and Kael kills him in the dark corridor. Aneb finds him.
- Scene 5: What Can't Be Washed. Wennefer is taken and freed by a masked stranger, Kael scrubs his hands, and the vent creaks.

## Epilogue — What the Sand Remembers
- The epilogue, "What the Sand Remembers." Kael wakes in the physician's chamber days later, scarred. Amenhotep publicly mourns his steward and announces that the steward was paid by someone inside the palace. That night, in the empty throne room, Kael tells Tutankhamun, Aneb and Nakhti the truth: he is eighteen, he came from the future, and a god cursed him. Kalici, hiding behind a column, steps out and stands with him. They swear the Nile Pact. At dawn, guards arrest Nakhti for planning the attack on the king, using papyrus "in his hand" as proof. He was framed.
- Nakhti being led away in chains at dawn while Kael stands in the empty throne room, unable to stop it.
- Kael, small, standing in the long shadow of the empty throne as dawn light comes through the doorway, with the Nile Pact water still wet on his hands.
- "They say gods don't make mistakes. Tonight I decided to become the one god of this age that Egypt can't ignore, child's face and all."
- Nakhti is imprisoned for a crime he didn't commit, and the team has to free him without exposing themselves. In the last extra scene, Amenhotep holds Kael's 10,000 yen bill up to a lamp and studies the foreign face printed on it.
