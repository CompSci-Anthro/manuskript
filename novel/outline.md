## Ch01 — The Wish Beneath the Cherry Blossoms
- s01 The Last Ordinary Morning (Japan, spring): cold-open flash of Thebes → "My parents were obsessed with history." Kael walks the cherry road; exams, Soren's unread texts, Mrs. Hayashi and the unpaid book order (10,000-yen bill into breast pocket). Kids playing; at the oldest tree's shrine he says "I wish I was ten again. Things were so much simpler." Crack — tree falls — dark.
- s02 The God of the Past (void of sundials, grey sand): the god tells him he died and heard the wish; grants it his own way; rules: two years, understood tongue, you keep what you carried nearest your heart; "Why me?" — the god echoes his mother's warning; "Simple? We shall see." Hourglass turned. Sunlight.
- s03 The Market at Thebes (noon): wakes in a lane; heat, smells; sees ten-year-old blond reflection in the bread seller's cracked blue jar. Hunger; offers the yen bill; crowd; Medjay. Runs: crowd, cart axle, drop down the quay wall into Nile mud; caught by a soldier. Dragged through the market reading the city. Clutching the bill.
- s04 Before the Throne (cell → throne room): thrown in a holding cell (illustration beat); hauled up; lotus columns, myrrh; a noble who questioned a royal decree is "weighed" and killed behind the columns. Kael thrown down, bill as evidence; officials call for the Weighing; Amenhotep silent; Nakhtmin a wall; Kael looks up: the pharaoh is nine. Eyes lock.
- s05 The Pharaoh's Decree: Tut takes the sentencing himself: "I shall sentence you. By my royal authority, I declare you my royal playmate and pet." Objection; "Pharaoh has sentenced." Amenhotep's eyes narrow. Messenger: Hatti takes another northern city — Kael knows how this reign ends. Nakhtmin cuts the rope; Tut's cold whisper ("You looked at him before you looked at me"). Dragged to a servants' room; soldier: the last playmate lasted eleven days. End: "I wished for a simpler life… and the gods gave me a palace full of people who want me dead."
