# Throne of Ashes — Outline
<!-- One line per chapter. Add scene beats only for the next chapter to write. -->

## Ch01 — The Wish Beneath the Cherry Blossoms (written outside the repo, Draft C.4, ~10,000 words)
Kael dies under the cherry tree, meets the God of the Past, wakes ten years old in Thebes, is arrested, watches the fan-bearer weighed and killed, and is sentenced by Tut to be his "royal playmate and pet." Ends in the servants' room: "One. Seven hundred and twenty-nine to go."

## Ch02 — The Shield of the Pharaoh (~6,500 words, one day, day 2)
Purpose: show that killing is routine around the king; bring in Aneb and Nakhti. Mood: tense. No comedy. Illustration: the councilor dying at the table.

### s01 — Games in the Royal Chambers (Tut's bedchamber, afternoon)
- Opens on Tut's voice: "Sit with me, sit with me." Aneb fetched Kael at noon; Kael counted the steps and turns.
- Senet: Kael loses slowly, as told. Aneb at the bed, never leaving Tut's side, watching the foreign pet with open jealousy; "Boy."
- Tut forgets to be king and laughs; quail eggs; Kael won't eat until Tut has. The carved boat, the bronze grate high in the ceiling (just noticed, nothing more).
- Kael starts to relax: maybe this second childhood won't be so bad. Hook: a shadow moves behind the far curtain.

### s02 — The Assassin (same chamber)
- The assassin bursts out with a blade. Kael freezes. Tut doesn't flinch; keeps holding his piece. Aneb runs; Nakhtmin is through the door faster and cuts the man down. Slow, vivid: the blade stops a hand's width from Tut.
- "That's the third this season." Exchange: "You didn't move." / "You didn't move either." / "I'm used to it." / "Your turn." Kael's hand shakes. "Who sent him?" "Someone who loves me very much, probably."
- Nakhtmin: "It will be done." Aneb's line: a slave, still a man. Aneb reads Kael's freeze as cowardice; Tut reads it as calm.
- Kael's realization: this is normal; the pet is an easier target; he knows nothing. He needs a teacher. Hook: he saw the blade stop before the khopesh landed.

### s03 — Finding Nakhti (scribes' archive, late afternoon)
- Who knows the palace and has no reason to lie to a toy? Someone the court spits on. Aneb's sneer gives him the name: "Go ask the Raven. He likes strays."
- The archive: ink, dust, a wine cup. Nakhti, one green eye, wolf tail, bare feet. Dismisses him with questions: why teach a foreign child?
- Kael's observation: the blade stopped before Nakhtmin touched him; a man who means it doesn't stop; he wanted to be seen trying. Nakhti: "And who did they blame?" Silence. Nakhti agrees: "Come to the hall tonight. Sit where the king puts you. Watch where I look."

### s04 — Watch This Common Occurrence (the dining hall, evening)
- Lamps, lotus bowls, harpists. Kael on a cushion at Tut's feet; Nakhti at the lowest table near the door. The Vizier at the king's right; the High Priest (unnamed); Userhat; Nakhtmin standing.
- Nakhti, passing: "Watch this common occurrence." Kael notices the councilor Kanefer's cup arrives already full on a tray, not poured from the jar.
- Kanefer drinks and dies, slowly and vividly, into the lotus bowl. Nobody stops eating. Harps go on. Amenhotep rises, takes the widow's hands in his wrapped ones and smiles warmly.
- Kael's face doesn't do what a child's would. The Vizier's gold eyes find him and stay. Tut touches his scarab and looks away.
- Corridor after, Nakhti: "This is the truth of the royal palace."

### s05 — The First Lesson (Nakhti's room, lamplight)
- Date stones on the floor for the powerful: who holds land (Amun's temple, the Vizier administers the king's), who controls the temples (the High Priest; Nakhti won't say his name: names are paid for here), who commands soldiers (Nakhtmin's guards at the palace, Horemheb far north, the Vizier's own men). Ay at Akhmim, keeping silent; Kael knows that name.
- Three truths: never show your real thoughts; never trust beauty or kindness; never forget the cost of each lie.
- Two factions. Beware: Userhat, the Vizier's steward, a man who comes and goes from the Vizier's house with his face hidden. Trust: few. Hori: "fed or ordered; find out which."
- Kael asks sharp questions and connects threads faster than Nakhti expects. Chapter-ending line, Nakhti: "You have the body of a child, but you listen like a man who has already lost something. Wisdom like that is rare, and in this palace, it's dangerous."

## Ch03 — The Girl Who Could Not Scream
Amenhotep's interlude (through Kalici's eyes): he orders her to frame Kael. Night: she can't scream; Kael asks "Who hurt you?"; they talk until dawn about her father the scribe.

## Ch04 — The Handmaid
Tut makes Kalici Kael's handmaid before witnesses, overruling the Vizier for the first time. A week later Tut confides that his father was poisoned. Kael's goal turns to hunting the poisoner.

## Ch05 — The Wager
Kael's wager with Nakhtmin; Nakhtmin starts teaching him to defend himself; the storeroom stakeout; the guard's token; Nakhti's list of the old king's last table.

## Ch06 — The Memorial Feast
The Vizier serves the old king's last dinner again as bait. Kael knocks the king's cup away; Amenhotep drinks from the same jar. "Who taught you to fear that cup, little one?"

## Ch07 — Kalici's Chapter
Kalici's POV: spying in the Vizier's house; the hooded man's scent, myrrh and burnt honey. Kael moves to the mat at the foot of Tut's bed.

## Ch08 — The Secret Council
The weekly secret meeting; the water-carrier who overheard; Kael kills him; Aneb finds him and asks "You're not a child. What are you?"; they hide the body. Wennefer named and taken.

## Ch09 — The Midnight Blade
Smoke, a false alarm, the steward's poisoned blade; Kael takes the stab for Tut; Ren drops through the vent, kills the steward, carries Kael to the physician: "I received a prophecy."

## Epilogue — What the Sand Remembers
Kael tells Tut, Aneb and Nakhti the truth; Kalici steps out and stays; the Nile Pact. Dawn: Nakhti arrested, framed. Extra scene: Amenhotep studies the ¥10,000 bill by lamplight.
